01 · About

A working farm, filmed as it actually happens

Khet Chalo follows daily life on a real farmhouse on the Yamuna Expressway belt, a few minutes from the upcoming Jewar international airport — vegetable rotation, dairy, goats, poultry, and the honest, unscripted rhythm of running it.

Every few days we also talk straight about the land and the investment story unfolding around this belt — no guaranteed-return promises, no hype. Just what's actually here.
